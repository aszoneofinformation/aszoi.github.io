ASZOI

Connecting work with the people it needs.

ASZOI is the communication and media interface of its wider institutional ecosystem, connecting operational work with the public, contributors, collaborators, and relevant opportunities.

01 / About

A communication interface for institutional work.

ASZOI exists to make institutional work visible, understandable, accessible, and connected to the people who can contribute to it.

We communicate the work of the wider ASZOI ecosystem, support its public presence, organise its publications and media, and create pathways through which people can discover where their knowledge, skills, resources, or interest may be useful.

Understand our purpose →
02 / Purpose

The right work should reach the right people.

Good work does not exist in isolation. It needs audiences, contributors, collaborators, institutional relationships, and opportunities to move forward.

ASZOI builds the communication layer that helps those connections happen.

05 / Publication

A public record of the work.

The ASZOI Publication Repository brings together the published record of work across the wider ecosystem.

It provides a structured route to reports, papers, records, publications, and other materials that should remain accessible beyond the moment of their release.

06 / Participate

There are many ways to take part.

Participation is not limited to one role. ASZOI connects people with the forms of contribution that best fit their interests, capabilities, and the needs of the work.

Contributors Bring knowledge, skills, ideas, or resources to relevant work. → Collaborators Work with the ecosystem on projects, initiatives, and institutional opportunities. → Researchers & practitioners Connect expertise with work where it can produce meaningful value. → Public participants Follow, discuss, question, share, and engage with the public-facing work. →
