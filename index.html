ASZOI develops systems for observing, investigating, analysing and communicating developments, information environments and complex realities.

About ASZOI
ASZOI is an independent institute in its foundation phase, building an integrated system for observation, project development, research, analysis and public communication. The institute aims to produce structured knowledge, documented findings and useful public resources.

How ASZOI Works

01

ASZOP

Observation and information collection. ASZOP focuses on identifying relevant developments, signals, patterns and data.

02

ASZON

Organisation and system development. ASZON examines whether observations and planned actions remain aligned with their purpose.

03

ASZOA

Analysis and outcome evaluation. ASZOA examines results, novelty, relevance and whether the work produces meaningful value.
