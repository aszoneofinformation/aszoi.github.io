01 / About ASZOI

A communication and media interface for institutional work.

ASZOI supports the communication, documentation, public representation, and external engagement of the wider ecosystem.

Rather than functioning as an operational unit itself, ASZOI provides the systems through which institutional work can be communicated clearly, presented publicly, and connected with people who may contribute to or engage with it.

Explore ASZOI →
02 / Purpose

Connecting meaningful work with the people, knowledge, and opportunities around it.

Important work can remain disconnected when information is difficult to discover, understand, or engage with.

ASZOI addresses this gap by developing communication channels, public-facing information, institutional relationships, and participation pathways that allow work to reach the audiences and contributors relevant to it.

05 / Publication

A public record of work, knowledge, and institutional output.

The Publication Repository brings together the public record of research, reports, briefs, institutional documents, and other material produced across the wider ecosystem.

Publications are organised as a continuing record through which the work can be discovered, referenced, and followed over time.
