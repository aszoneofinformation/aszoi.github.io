01 / ABOUT ASZOI

A communication and media interface for institutional work.

ASZOI connects the work of the wider institutional ecosystem with the people who need to know about it, contribute to it, collaborate with it, or engage with its outcomes.

Its role is communication and media: shaping public-facing information, supporting institutional representation, documenting work, managing relationships, and creating meaningful pathways for participation.

Explore ASZOI →
02 / PURPOSE

The right work should reach the right people.

Meaningful work can lose its value when people cannot find it, understand it, contribute to it, or connect it with relevant opportunities.

ASZOI exists at that interface. It helps turn institutional work into clear public communication and creates the channels through which people can discover and engage with it.
