ASZOI develops institutional systems for observing, investigating, analysing and communicating developments, information environments and complex realities.

About ASZOI
ASZOI is an independent institute currently in its foundation phase. It is building an integrated system for projects, publications, information, analysis and public communication. The institute's work is organised into documented projects and outputs that can be examined through the ASZOI public record.

How ASZOI Works

Institutional System
01

ASZOP

Observation and information collection. Identifying relevant developments, signals, patterns and data.

02

ASZON

Organisation and system oversight. Examining planning, execution and alignment throughout institutional work.

03

ASZOA

Analysis and evaluation. Examining results, relevance, novelty and whether work produces meaningful value.
